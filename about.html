About

Built from curiosity.

I’m Emmanuel Odoemelam, a Senior Product Designer with over five years of experience taking products from early ideas to launch across fintech, AI, healthcare, gaming, and Web3.

I studied engineering, but somewhere along the way, I found myself drawn to design.

My journey began with visual design, mostly thinking about colours, layouts, and how things looked. But the more products I worked on, the more curious I became about everything beyond the screen.

Why did people trust one product and abandon another? Why did some flows feel effortless while others felt confusing? And how could design do more than make things look good?

Those questions pulled me into product design.

Since then, I’ve worked closely with founders and engineers to turn early ideas into real products. I enjoy the uncertain stage most, when the problem is still taking shape, and there is room to ask better questions, challenge assumptions, and influence what gets built.

My engineering background still shows up in how I work. I like understanding how systems connect, breaking complicated problems into smaller parts, and finding ways to make them feel simple for the people using them.

Outside of work, I spend time mentoring designers, experimenting with AI tools, and building ideas that may or may not become real products.
