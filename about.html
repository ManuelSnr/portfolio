About

Designer who builds,
not just delivers.

I'm Emmanuel — a senior product designer based in Lagos, Nigeria. For the past 5+ years I've worked with early-stage founders across fintech, healthtech, AI, Web3, and social gaming to take products from zero to one.

I care deeply about designing for trust — the kind of design that makes users feel safe giving you their money, their health data, or their time. That means being precise where it matters, and getting out of the way everywhere else.

Outside of work I mentor designers on ADPList, think about gaming interfaces, and build things for fun.
