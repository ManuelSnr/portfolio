About

I'm a senior product designer based in Lagos, Nigeria. Over the past five years I've worked with founders and engineers at the early stage, where design decisions carry the most weight.

A recurring theme in my work is designing for trust — KYC flows, financial transactions, healthcare scheduling, and real-money gaming mechanics where clarity and reliability are non-negotiable. I'm comfortable in regulated environments and fluent in the space between design and engineering.

Beyond execution, I help teams build better practices: design systems, PRD writing, engineering handoff, and mentoring designers through ADPList (3,000+ minutes logged).
