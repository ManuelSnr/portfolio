Intro

I’m Emmanuel, a Senior Product Designer based in Nigeria.

I studied engineering, which probably explains why I’ve always been interested in how things work. I liked breaking problems down, understanding the parts, and figuring out how they could work better.

That mindset still shapes how I approach design. I’m drawn to the whole product lifecycle, especially the messy 0 to 1 stage, when the problem is still taking shape, the trade-offs are real, and design can influence far more than the interface.

I enjoy turning that uncertainty into products that feel simple, useful, and trustworthy.
